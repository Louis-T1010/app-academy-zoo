GEMSBOK

The gemsbok or gemsbuck (Oryx gazella) is a large antelope in the Oryx genus. It is native to the arid regions of Southern Africa, such as the Kalahari Desert. Some authorities formerly included the East African oryx as a subspecies.

The gemsbok is depicted on the coat of arms of Namibia, where the current population of the species is estimated at 373,000 individuals. The name "gemsbok" in English is derived from Afrikaans, which itself comes from the Dutch name of the male chamois. Despite some superficial facial pattern similarities, the chamois and the oryx are not closely related.
