GORILLA

Gorillas are ground-dwelling, predominantly herbivorous apes that inhabit the forests of central Africa. The genus Gorilla is divided into two species: eastern gorillas and western gorillas, and several subspecies. They are the largest living primates by size. Gorillas share 95 to 99% of their DNA with humans, making them our closest relatives after chimpanzees and bonobos.

Gorillas live in tropical or subtropical forests, from lowland swamps to mountain cloud forests. Mountain gorillas inhabit altitudes from 2,200 to 4,300 metres in the Virunga Volcanoes. Western lowland gorillas live in Central West Africa, while eastern lowland gorillas are found in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
