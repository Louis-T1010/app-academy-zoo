WARTHOG

Phacochoerus is a genus of wild pigs in the family Suidae, known as warthogs. It is the only genus in the subfamily Phacochoerinae. Found in open and semi-open habitats across sub-Saharan Africa, they are adapted to arid regions. The two species are the desert warthog (Phacochoerus aethiopicus) and the common warthog (Phacochoerus africanus).

Warthogs are covered in bristly hairs, though their bodies and heads appear mostly naked from a distance. Males have distinct facial wattles and long curved tusks that can reach up to 63.5 cm (25 in). Warthogs are primarily herbivorous but may occasionally consume small animals. The Cape warthog, a desert warthog subspecies, became extinct around 1865.
